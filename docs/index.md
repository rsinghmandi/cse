# Core 
- Baseline -- Clear the exam  
    - this is like a higher level board exam. Not even like a university exam.
    - its straight and defined -- starts with preparation and ends with mains paper.
    - have to have targeted approach. PYQ is the target.
    - so, take it as a project -- one that has a deadline.
- There is too much noise in the market. Don't fall trap to the FOMO feeling.
- Limit the base(static) resources and do it again and again. 
    - Refer to extra resources where the base is insufficient only for those topics.
    - Optimization is the key.
- How you write is more important than how much you read. The benchmark is toppers' answer sheets. You have to put all you know in 3 hrs. How much can u put in 3 hrs? This is a test with contraints.
- Optional decides the success or failure. GS marks are more or less similar.
- Design a strategy that suits you and your situation.
- Focus on strengths and manage the weaknesses.
